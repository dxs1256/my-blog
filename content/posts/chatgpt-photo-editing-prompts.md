周末清理相册，翻出一堆旅行照：天空过曝、人像发黄、背景全是路人。打开电脑想修个图，PS 一个图层就能劝退我。

后来刷到一条推文，说 **ChatGPT** 现在可以直接编辑照片了——不用会 PS，上传照片 + 说人话，AI 就按你的要求修。帖子里附了 10 条提示词，从人像精修到产品摄影全覆盖，我直接全抄了下来。

## 🎯 背景：ChatGPT 从"画图"到"修图"

ChatGPT 的图片能力早就从文生图升级到了"上传照片 + 自然语言编辑"。9 月发布的 Images 2.5 把编辑指令的遵循能力又提了一档：保留参考图主体、多轮对话连续修改、生成延迟比上一代降低一半左右。

这意味着以前要在 Lightroom 里拖十几分钟滑块的活儿——调曝光、压高光、修瑕疵、换背景——现在一句话就能交代出去。

## 📋 10 条提示词，按场景取用

提示词的核心技巧就一句：**说清楚"改什么"和"千万别动什么"**。下面按场景分类整理，直接复制到 ChatGPT 里用。

### 人像类

**1/ 专业人像精修**

> Edit this photo like a professional portrait photographer. Improve lighting, balance skin tones, remove minor blemishes naturally, sharpen the eyes, and add subtle background blur. Preserve my real facial features and natural skin texture.

翻译成人话：提亮补光、平衡肤色、自然去瑕疵、眼神锐化、背景虚化——但明确要求保留真实五官和皮肤质感。最后一句是灵魂，删了修出来的就是一张"假脸"。

![人像精修效果示意](https://i.ibb.co/DD5sSZkZ/b8903bdcb36e.jpg)

**4/ LinkedIn 头像**

> Turn this casual photo into a professional LinkedIn headshot. Use soft studio lighting, a clean neutral background, natural skin retouching, and polished business photography. Keep my face, identity, and expression authentic.

随手一张生活照，换成干净的商务头像，适合懒得去照相馆的朋友。

### 风格类

**2/ 电影感**

> Transform this photo into a cinematic movie still. Apply dramatic lighting, rich shadows, professional color grading, and subtle film grain. Create a premium Hollywood-inspired atmosphere while keeping the subject realistic.

**3/ DSLR 相机质感**

> Make this photo look like it was captured with a high-end DSLR camera and an 85mm portrait lens. Enhance sharpness, create realistic depth of field, improve exposure, and produce beautiful background bokeh without changing the subject.

手机拍的，想要"单反 85mm 镜头"的虚化背景——这条就是干这个的。

**5/ 黄金时刻**

> Edit this photo to look like it was taken during golden hour. Add warm sunset lighting, soft highlights, natural shadows, and beautiful golden tones. Maintain realistic skin colors and preserve all original details.

把随手拍的照片调成日落前一小时的暖色调，整张照片立刻有氛围。

**7/ 时尚大片**

> Transform this photo into a luxury fashion magazine editorial. Apply sophisticated studio lighting, refined contrast, elegant color grading, and professional skin retouching. Preserve my original outfit, facial features, and realistic proportions.

### 清理修复类

**6/ 移除杂物**

> Remove distracting objects, unwanted people, and visual clutter from the background of this photo. Reconstruct the missing areas naturally using surrounding textures, lighting, and perspective. Keep the main subject completely unchanged.

旅游照里全是路人？这条是"照片版去人术"。

**8/ 老照片修复**

> Restore this old or blurry photograph with professional precision. Reduce noise, repair scratches, improve clarity, recover faded details, and correct discoloration. Preserve original facial features and avoid inventing missing details.

注意最后一句：**不要凭空编造缺失细节**——这是修旧照片的安全底线，宁可保留瑕疵也别给老人"换脸"。

### 商业类

**9/ 产品摄影**

> Transform this product photo into a premium commercial advertisement. Improve lighting, eliminate distracting reflections, create realistic shadows, and use a clean luxury background. Preserve the product's exact shape, colors, branding, and labels.

![产品摄影效果示意](https://i.ibb.co/XrdsvVhx/aa745ddce62f.jpg)

做电商、挂闲鱼的都能用：一碗泡面也能修出旗舰店广告的质感。

### 旅行类

**10/ 旅行摄影增强**

> Edit this travel photo like a professional National Geographic photographer. Enhance landscape details, balance sky and foreground exposure, improve natural colors, and add subtle atmospheric depth. Keep the scenery realistic without adding artificial elements.

![旅行摄影效果示意](https://i.ibb.co/kgkhMQ9j/2e04835104a0.jpg)

原帖最后一条，也是我最想试的一条。天空过曝、地面太暗是手机旅拍的常见痛点，这条专门处理"天地曝光平衡"，还特别强调不添加人造元素、保持真实。

## 💡 怎么用效果最好

几条实测经验：

1. **先传图再发指令**：在同一个对话里上传照片，再粘贴提示词，别开新对话
2. **一次只改一个方向**：学提示词的写法，一条提示词聚焦一个目标，别又调色又抠图又换背景
3. **"保留/不改变"是安全词**：所有提示词里 Preserve、Keep 后面的内容都是保命条款，删了就等于让 AI 放飞自我
4. **英文提示词直接用**：ChatGPT 对英文指令的理解更稳，嫌长可以删形容词，核心动词别动

原帖发出来十几个小时，收藏量是点赞的两倍还多，评论区也已经有人实测反馈"确实管用"——这种提示词就该先收藏再用。

下一步：挑你手里最拿得出手的一张照片，丢进 ChatGPT，贴上对应场景的提示词，对比一下修图前后。
